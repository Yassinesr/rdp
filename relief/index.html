Photo tips

How to get the perfect "good boy" shot.

  • Face the camera, with both eyes visible
  • Squeak a toy behind your phone for perky ears
  • Get down to their eye level rather than shooting from above
  • Daylight is best: no flash, no filters
  • Get close: head and chest fill the frame
  • One pet per photo, uncropped if you can

Old or imperfect photos usually work, too, especially for memorial pieces. If one won't work, we'll tell you before we make anything.

FAQ

Questions from fellow pet parents.

Will it really look like my pet?

Before anything is made, we email you a preview of the sculpted portrait. If something's off, like the ears, markings or expression, tell us and we'll fix it. One change is free. We only start once you approve.

How long until it arrives?

Preview within 2 business days, sculpted and painted in 3–5 business days after you approve, then 7–12 business days in the mail with tracking. Most orders arrive in 11–19 business days. Need it by a date? Check the "order by" date next to the price.

What's the difference from the Portrait Tag?

The Relief Keychain has a deeper sculpted portrait set in a faux-leather frame on a braided strap, at $49. The 3D Portrait Tag is a full-color raised portrait with a theme and a nameplate, at $29. Add a matching tag to your order for +$19.

Can I order for two pets?

Yes. Choose 2 pets and add a photo and name for each. Each pet gets their own keychain, and the second one costs less.

Can you ship it straight to someone as a gift?

Yes. Enter their address at checkout and add a gift note. It arrives in the gift box with your card and no prices inside.

Is it a good memorial gift?

Many people choose it in memory of a pet who has passed. Take your time with the preview, and we'll treat their photo with care.

How do I look after it?

Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid soaking it, and keep it out of strong sun for long periods so the colors stay bright.
