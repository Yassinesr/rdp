Photo tips

The photo that makes the best portrait.

  • Face the camera, with both eyes visible
  • Good daylight, no flash and no filters
  • Close-up of the head and chest, not the whole room
  • One pet per photo, uncropped if you can
  • Old photos are fine for memorial pieces. We'll work with what you have

Not sure your photo is good enough? Upload it anyway. If it won't work, we'll tell you before we make anything.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will it really look like my pet?

Before anything is made, we email you a preview of the 3D portrait. If something's off, such as the ears, markings or expression, tell us and we'll fix it. One revision is free. We only start production once you approve.

How long until it arrives?

Preview within 2 business days, made in 3–5 business days after you approve, then 7–12 business days in the mail with tracking. Most orders arrive in 11–19 business days. Need it by a date? Check the "order by" date above.

Can I order for more than one pet?

Yes. Choose 2 or 3 pets and add a photo and name for each. Every pet gets its own portrait.

Can you ship it straight to someone as a gift?

Yes. Enter their address at checkout and add a gift note. It arrives in the gift box with your card, and there are no prices or invoices inside.

Is it a good memorial gift?

Many people choose the Rainbow theme in memory of a pet who has passed. Older or imperfect photos usually work. Take your time with the preview, and we'll treat their photo with care.

What if it arrives damaged or doesn't look right?

Send us a photo within 30 days of delivery and we'll remake it or refund you, whichever you prefer.

How do I look after it?

Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid soaking it, and keep it out of strong sun for long periods so the colors stay bright.
