Photo tips

How to get the perfect "good boy" shot.

  • Face the camera, with both eyes visible
  • Squeak a toy behind your phone for perky ears
  • Daylight is best: no flash, no filters
  • Get close: head and chest fill the frame
  • One pet per photo, uncropped if you can

Old or imperfect photos usually work, too, especially for memorial pieces. If one won't work, we'll tell you before we make anything.

FAQ

Questions from fellow pet parents.

Will it really look like my pet?

Before anything is made, we email you a preview of the 3D portrait. If something's off, like the ears, markings or expression, tell us and we'll fix it. One change is free. We only start once you approve.

How long until it arrives?

Preview within 2 business days, made in 3–5 business days after you approve, then 7–12 business days in the mail with tracking. Most orders arrive in 11–19 business days. Need it by a date? Check the "order by" date next to the price.

Can I order for more than one pet?

Yes. Choose 2 or 3 pets and add a photo and name for each. Every pet gets their own tag, and the more you add, the less each one costs.

What's the difference from the Relief Keychain?

The Portrait Tag is a full-color raised portrait with a theme and a nameplate, at $29. The Sculpted Relief Keychain has a deeper sculpted portrait set in a faux-leather frame on a braided strap, at $49. You can add one to your order for +$39.

Can you ship it straight to someone as a gift?

Yes. Enter their address at checkout and add a gift note. It arrives in the gift box with your card and no prices inside.

Is it a good memorial gift?

Many people choose the Rainbow theme in memory of a pet who has passed. Take your time with the preview, and we'll treat their photo with care.

How do I look after it?

Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid soaking it, and keep it out of strong sun for long periods so the colors stay bright.
