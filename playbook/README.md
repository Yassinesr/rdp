# TikTok shop playbook

A working playbook for running a small online shop that sells to US (and UK/EU) shoppers through
TikTok, operated from China. Written November 2026 from public sources. Every number has a source
link or is labelled as an estimate, and the guides flag where sources conflict.

> **This repo is public.** Anyone can read these files, including competitors. If that matters,
> make the repo private (GitHub Pages on a private repo needs a paid GitHub plan), or keep your own
> numbers (supplier prices, margins) in a private copy.

## The business in one page

**How money is made.** A shopper sees a TikTok video, taps through to a product page, and buys.
For each order you keep the price, minus product cost, shipping and duties, payment or platform
fees, refunds and the cost of getting the sale (creator commission or ads). See the
[cost model](02-cost-model.md).

**What decides success, in order:**
1. **Shipping weight and box size.** Shipping a parcel from China to the US with duties paid costs
   roughly $11–18 for 0.5 kg and about $26 for 1 kg on one economy line
   ([NextSmartShip](https://www.nextsmartship.com/blog/ship-from-china-to-us/),
   [Fishgoo](https://blog.fishgoo.com/china-shipping-cost-estimator/)). Light, small products
   (under about 300 g) are the only ones that work shipped straight from China. Heavier ones need
   bulk import to a US warehouse.
2. **Cost of a sale.** TikTok ads averaged about **$32.74 per purchase** for ecommerce brands in
   2025. That's a Triple Whale figure, reported in 2026 benchmark roundups such as
   [Benly](https://benly.ai/learn/tiktok-ads/tiktok-ads-cost-benchmarks) and
   [Lebesgue](https://lebesgue.io/tiktok-ads/tiktok-ads-benchmarks-for-ctr-cr-and-cpm).
   A product earning $10–15 per order before ads can't pay that. Low-price products must sell
   through **free (organic) videos** and **creators paid per sale** (about 13% commission on
   average, per 2026 affiliate roundups such as
   [Hamster Garage](https://www.hamstergarage.com/article/tiktok-shop-affiliate-statistics-benchmarks-roi)
   and [TTCalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/tiktok-shop-commission-rates/)),
   and use paid ads only on proven videos.
3. **Order value.** Bundles (2–3 units, or product + accessory) raise the order value without
   adding much shipping cost.
4. **Content.** On TikTok, the video is the product page. Unpolished demos that show the result in
   the first 2–3 seconds win. See 2026 creative guides such as
   [Stackmatix](https://stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-creative-best-practices-2026) and
   [Megadigital](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/tiktok-ad-creative/).

**Decision flow for a new product:**

```
Idea → hard filters (weight, batteries/liquids, regulated, IP)          → 01-product-research
     → cost check: contribution before ads ≥ $15 at a realistic price   → 02-cost-model
     → samples + 5–10 organic videos over 7–10 days                     → 07-launch-and-operations
     → winner? → product page + checkout + creator outreach             → 03, 04
               → scale: Spark Ads on best videos, then US warehouse     → 04, 05
     → no winner? → kill it, next product
```

## Keeping it current

Fees, tariffs and TikTok rules change often, and several 2026 figures already conflict between
sources. Before you commit money, check the official page linked in each guide: TikTok Seller
Center, Shopify pricing, the USITC tariff database, and your shipping line's current quote. Then
update the numbers here.
