# 7. Launch and operations

A 30-day plan to get from zero to first sales, then the weekly routine and the numbers that tell
you whether to scale a product or drop it. Thresholds below are working rules for a small shop,
not published benchmarks. Tune them with your own data.

## 7.1 Before day 1: accounts and setup

- [ ] TikTok business account for the brand (one per brand, product-focused content only)
- [ ] Shopify store on Basic; policies published (see [guide 3](03-store-and-website.md#36-policies-and-pages-checklist))
- [ ] Payments working: PayPal now; plan Shopify Payments via a Hong Kong company later ([guide 6](06-legal-tax-payments.md#66-getting-paid))
- [ ] Payout account (Payoneer, LianLian, WorldFirst or PingPong) linked
- [ ] Domain pointed at the product pages; `CONFIG.store` and the variant IDs set in this repo
- [ ] TikTok Pixel ID in `assets/app.js`; TikTok app installed in Shopify; test purchase seen in Test Events
- [ ] 2–3 written quotes from duties-paid shipping lines ([guide 2](02-cost-model.md#25-shipping-quote-checklist-ask-every-forwarder-or-line))
- [ ] Cost model filled in for each product with real quotes

## 7.2 The 30-day launch plan

| Days | Goal | Do this | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Pick 2–3 products | Run the scorecard ([guide 1](01-product-research.md#13-scorecard-for-products-that-pass-the-filters)); get supplier quotes; order samples | 2–3 products score 70+ and clear $15 contribution |
| 4–7 | Samples in hand | Weigh and measure; check quality; film 10+ raw clips per product (hands, pets, close-ups, reactions) | Each product has a clip library |
| 8–17 | Organic test | Post 1–3 videos a day, each with 3 opening variants; reply to every comment | One video clearly beats the rest, with buying-intent comments |
| 12–17 | Page ready | Add the winner's product page (data file + build, or copy the existing pages); real video in the hero; link in bio | Test order completes on a phone |
| 18–24 | Creators | Send samples to 10–20 small creators (5k–100k followers) with matching audiences; offer a commission (10–20%) or a flat fee for 1–3 videos | 3+ creator videos live |
| 25–30 | First paid test (optional) | Boost the best organic or creator video with Spark Ads at a small daily budget; stop if cost per sale passes break-even (cost model) | Cost per sale known; scale or stop |

## 7.3 Weekly routine

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Daily (15 min) | Check orders are dispatched within 3 business days; upload tracking; answer messages within 24 h; reply to comments |
| Daily | Post 1–3 videos; save every comment question as a future video idea |
| Monday | Numbers review (7.4); decide scale, hold or kill for each product |
| Wednesday | Creator outreach and sample sends; reorder stock if needed |
| Friday | Plan next week's videos from the best performers' openings; check the [promotion calendar](04-tiktok-channels-and-ads.md#45-promotion-calendar-november-2026-to-february-2027) |
| Monthly | Update supplier and shipping quotes, fees and duties in the cost model; check TikTok, Shopify and tariff changes |

## 7.4 The numbers to track

| Metric | What it tells you | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 3-second hold rate / average watch time | Is the opening working? | TikTok analytics |
| Saves and shares per 1,000 views | Real interest (better than likes) | TikTok analytics |
| Link or profile clicks | Buying intent | TikTok analytics, UTM in Shopify |
| Page conversion rate (orders ÷ product page visits) | Is the page and offer convincing? Paid-traffic benchmark about 1.5–1.9% ([Lebesgue](https://lebesgue.io/tiktok-ads/tiktok-ads-benchmarks-for-ctr-cr-and-cpm)) | Shopify analytics |
| Average order value | Are bundles working? | Shopify |
| Contribution per order | Price − product − shipping − fees − refunds | Cost model with real numbers |
| Cost per sale (ads / creators) | Is getting sales affordable? | TikTok Ads, creator payouts |
| Dispatch time, delivery time | Customer experience and payment-provider risk | Shopify, shipping-line tracking |
| Refund and complaint rate | Quality or expectation problems | Shopify, inbox |

## 7.5 Scale, hold or kill

**Scale** when all are true:
- profit per order (after creator or ad cost) is positive for 2 weeks
- refund rate is under 5% for rigid items
- delivery time stays within the promise

Then:
1. Add creators, and boost proven videos with Spark Ads.
2. Raise order value with a 3-pack or an add-on.
3. When volume and weight justify it, run the US-warehouse numbers ([guide 5](05-sourcing-and-fulfillment.md#when-to-move-stock-to-a-us-warehouse)).
4. Consider TikTok Shop UK, then US with US stock ([guide 4](04-tiktok-channels-and-ads.md#41-which-tiktok-channel-from-china)).

**Hold** (fix one thing, then retest for a week) when:
- **Videos get interest but few sales.** Fix the page: real video, price, bundle, delivery promise.
- **Sales but thin profit.** Fix costs: packaging size, shipping line, supplier price break.

**Kill** when:
- about 10 videos plus 3 creator videos produce no standout
- or contribution can't reach $15 even after negotiating shipping and product cost
- or refunds or complaints keep running above about 10%

## 7.6 Customer service templates

**Where is my order?**
> Hi [name], thanks for your order! It shipped on [date] and is on its way. Tracking: [link].
> Delivery usually takes 7–12 business days, so it should arrive by [date]. I'll keep an eye on it
> and let you know if anything changes.

**Damaged or wrong item**
> I'm sorry about that! Could you send a quick photo? I'll send a replacement or a full refund,
> whichever you prefer. No need to return it.

**Refund within the guarantee window**
> No problem. I've refunded $[amount] to your original payment method; it can take 5–10 days to
> show. Thanks for giving us a try.

**Asking for feedback** (neutral, never conditional on a positive review)
> How's your [product] working out? If you have a minute, an honest review or a quick video helps
> other pet owners decide, and we read every one.

## 7.7 Tools stack (minimum)

| Need | Tool | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Store, checkout | Shopify Basic | $29–39/mo |
| Product pages | This repo on GitHub Pages | Free |
| Trend research | TikTok Creative Center (free) + one tracker (EchoTik / FastMoss / Kalodata) | $0–45/mo |
| Sourcing | 1688, Yiwu market, CJ Dropshipping | Free to use |
| Shipping | Duties-paid line (quotes) or CJPacket | Per parcel |
| Payouts | Payoneer / LianLian / WorldFirst / PingPong | Fees vary |
| Video | Phone + free editor (CapCut) | Free |
| Accounting | Spreadsheet or bookkeeping app + an accountant | Varies |
