# 4. TikTok channels and ads

Where to sell (TikTok Shop vs your own store), how sales get made (organic videos, creators,
paid ads), and what each costs.

## 4.3 How sales get made, cheapest first

### 1. Organic videos on your own account

The cheapest test there is: post product videos and watch the response. TikTok's own 2026 trend
report says shoppers respond to real, unpolished content (**"Reali-TEA"**), curiosity before the
pitch (**"Curiosity Detours"**), and emotional payoff (**"Emotional ROI"**)
([TikTok Next 2026](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_Next_2026_Trend_Report.pdf),
[Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-shares-2026-trend-predictions-for-marketers/809651/)).

**Posting plan while testing a product** (a working plan, not a benchmark): 1–3 videos a day for
7–10 days. Each core video gets 3 different openings.

### 2. Creators paid per sale (TikTok Shop affiliates)

On TikTok Shop, creators add your product to their videos and earn a commission on each sale.
The US average is about 13%: beauty 15–25%, home 10–20% (sources above). You pay **only when it
sells**, so for cheap products this is the best paid channel. Send free samples to creators whose
audiences match. On your own store, use an affiliate app or discount codes instead.

**Disclosure:** creators must label paid or gifted posts (TikTok's branded-content toggle or #ad).
That falls under the FTC's endorsement rules, which are separate from its fake-reviews rule (see
[guide 6](06-legal-tax-payments.md#68-advertising-and-consumer-law)).

### 3. Spark Ads (boost a video that already works)

Spark Ads boost an existing organic post, yours or a creator's with their permission, and keep its
likes and comments visible ([Megadigital](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/tiktok-ad-creative/)).
Use them **only on videos that already proved themselves organically**.

### 4. Standard paid ads

Benchmarks for ecommerce: CPM about $4–9, CPC about $0.80–1.00, CTR about 0.61%, conversion
1.56–1.92%, and an average cost per purchase of about $33 in 2025
([Lebesgue](https://lebesgue.io/tiktok-ads/tiktok-ads-benchmarks-for-ctr-cr-and-cpm),
[Benly](https://benly.ai/learn/tiktok-ads/tiktok-ads-cost-benchmarks)). From those click costs and
conversion rates, expect **about $40–60 per sale on cold traffic** until you have proven videos. Run
the [cost model](02-cost-model.md) first: if contribution before ads is under about $30, cold paid
ads will lose money.

**Account setup (2026):**
- Advertisers verify with a business license, tax registration or government letter. Licenses can't
  be borrowed or rented ([TikTok document help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/uploading-documents-for-advertisers-on-tiktok?lang=zh)).
- Ads are reported to need a **verified account** linked through Business Center from early 2026
  ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/tiktok-kills-custom-identity-as-brands-must-link-verified-accounts/)).
- Landing pages must show local-currency prices plus terms, shipping, privacy, returns and refund
  policies ([TikTok ad policy](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-advertising-policies-ad-creatives-landing-page-ad-format-and-functionality?lang=en)).

I found no TikTok document on rules specific to mainland China advertisers. Many China-based
sellers use a TikTok-authorized agency account, so ask TikTok support or an official agency before
committing.

## 4.4 Making videos that sell

Common ground across 2026 creative guides
([Stackmatix](https://stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-creative-best-practices-2026),
[Megadigital](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/tiktok-ad-creative/),
[Creatify](https://creatify.ai/blog/tiktok-ads-complete-guide-to-creating-high-performing-creatives-in-2026)):

- **Hook in the first 3 seconds.** Show the result within about 2 seconds rather than building up
  to it.
- **Hook types that work for products:**
  - Problem: "my dog hates baths"
  - Result first: the necklace with their name, on camera
  - POV: "POV: your cat finally stopped sitting on your keyboard"
  - Demo first: the product working, no intro
  - Proof: real comments or reactions, once you have them
- **Look native:** shot on a phone, real hands, real pets, on-screen text, trending sound used
  lightly. Polished brand ads tend to underperform.
- **Test openings, not whole videos:** one core video with 3–5 different first 3 seconds.
- **End with one clear action:** "tap the link", "comment 'name' and I'll show you more".

**Video ideas by product:**

| Product | 3 starter videos |
|---|---|
| Personalized necklace | Unboxing the gift reaction · "I got my mom's handwriting engraved" · 3 names, 3 styles |
| Charm claw clips | 5 looks in 10 seconds · Build-your-own charm set · "Clips that match my outfit" |
| Lick mat | Bath before vs after · Freeze-it tip · Fireworks night setup |
| Fascia ring / stretching strap | 30-second desk stretch · Before/after range of motion · "What I use after the gym" |
