product that sells on TikTok, ships cheaply from China, and earns **at least $15 per order before
ads** ([cost rule](../02-cost-model.md#21-the-formulas)).

**Result: lead with a custom pet keepsake** (the customer's pet photo made into a keychain or
necklace). It fits the Pawse pet brand, weighs 20–40 g, costs about $2–8 to buy, and sells for
$18–60 in the US. After the Pinduoduo price check (section 6b), the hero product will be **either
a 3D leather keychain or a stainless necklace**, chosen after samples. Keep the lick mat as the
second product. Seven other candidates were dropped (section 4).

## 6. Recommended product: custom pet photo necklace

**Pitch:** "Your pet, engraved." The customer uploads a photo of their dog or cat and gets it
laser-engraved on a stainless pendant, with the name on the back.

**Why it fits:**
- **Brand:** fits Pawse and the existing site (the lick mat stays as a second product).
- **Profit:** contribution of about $16–25 per order at $34.99, and higher with a 2-pet version or
  a gift box.
- **Video:** emotional reveal videos ("I engraved my dog who passed", "gift for a dog mom") match
  TikTok's own "Emotional ROI" trend
  ([TikTok Next 2026](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_Next_2026_Trend_Report.pdf)).
- **Seasons:** year-round memorial gift, plus Christmas, Valentine's Day (14 Feb 2027) and
  Mother's Day (9 May 2027, a big "dog mom" moment).

**Risks to manage:**
- **Engraving quality from phone photos is the main risk.** Test with good and poor photos before
  selling.
- **Competition:** the same item is $4–8 on AliExpress. Your edge is TikTok content,
  photo-preparation quality, gift packaging and speed.
- **Longer delivery:** custom production (2–5 days) + shipping (7–12 business days). The
  **Christmas 2026 cutoff is about 3–5 December**, so the realistic first target is Valentine's Day.
- **Photo workflow:** the store needs a photo-upload step, and each order's photo has to reach the
  supplier. That needs a Shopify upload app or a "send us your photo" email step. To build later.
- **Material claims:** say "stainless steel" only if the supplier confirms it (ask for 316L), and
  get nickel and lead test reports for the EU.

**How it's made (seller screenshot):** the seller builds a **3D model from the customer's photo**
on screen, e.g. a kitten whose paws break out of the coin frame, before production. So:
- **A proof step exists.** Offer customers "approve your pet's portrait before we make it" (a
  selling point, and fewer refunds). Set a rule: approve within 24 h, or one free revision, then
  auto-approve.
- **Lead time** = modeling + printing + painting. Ask how many working days, and how many
  revisions are included.
- **Probably resin** (3D-printed, then painted or UV-printed). Raised parts can chip in transit, so
  ship in a small rigid box with padding, not just a plastic bag. Recheck the billed weight with
  the box.
- Pop-out designs (paws breaking the frame) are very strong for short videos.

**Still unproven until samples are in hand:** scratch resistance of the raised surface, color
fading, the leather smell, real weight, production days, and how the seller turns a phone photo
into the artwork (do they send a proof?).
