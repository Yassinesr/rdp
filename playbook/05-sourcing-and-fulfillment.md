# 5. Sourcing and fulfillment

How to find reliable suppliers, get products to US shoppers, and know when to switch from shipping
each order from China to keeping stock in a US warehouse.

## 5.1 Where to source (and your edge as a China-based seller)

| Source | Good for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **1688.com** | Lowest prices, huge range | Filter for **跨境专供** (made for export) and **一件代发** (dropshipping). Message in Chinese on Wangwang. The desk-perch supplier, for example, listed Amazon and independent stores as its usual outlets |
| **Yiwu market (义乌国际商贸城)** | Jewelry, hair accessories, phone charms, small goods | Go in person, handle the product, negotiate. Your biggest advantage over overseas dropshippers |
| **Alibaba.com** | English-speaking export suppliers, custom/OEM | Higher prices than 1688; useful for custom packaging and logos |
| **CJ Dropshipping** | Hands-off fulfillment, Shopify integration, some US warehouse stock | Free to use; CJPacket about 7–15 days to the US; US warehouse delivery about 2–8 business days; US outbound fee about $0.50/order up to 1 kg; overseas stock needs at least 10 per variant and 100 in total ([Spocket](https://www.spocket.co/blogs/cjdropshipping-overview), [Revenue Geeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/cjdropshipping-pricing/)). These figures are from third-party reviews, so check your CJ account |

## 5.2 Supplier vetting checklist

- [ ] Years on 1688, ratings, repeat-buyer rate (回头率), sales volume (成交)
- [ ] Factory or trading company? Factories are cheaper; traders offer more variety
- [ ] **Samples first:** check quality, real weight and box size (they decide shipping cost)
- [ ] Price breaks: 1 piece (dropship) vs 50 / 100 / 500
- [ ] Dropshipping support: blind shipping (no supplier invoice or ads in the box), your own insert card
- [ ] Custom logo or packaging, and the minimum order for it
- [ ] Test reports where relevant:
  - food-contact silicone: FDA 21 CFR 177.2600 (US) / LFGB (EU)
  - jewelry: nickel release (EU REACH), lead and cadmium
- [ ] Permission to use their photos and videos until you have your own
- [ ] Load-test or safety evidence for anything that holds weight
- [ ] Dispatch time (发货时间) and who pays if items are defective or lost

**Wangwang template** (edit per product):

> 您好，我做跨境一件代发（美国），想确认：1）单件和批量价格；2）单个包装尺寸和毛重，能否压缩包装；
> 3）是否支持一件代发和无痕发货（不放发票/广告）；4）可否定制LOGO/包装，起订量多少；
> 5）有无相关检测报告；6）能否授权使用产品图片和视频。谢谢！

In English, this asks for: single-unit and bulk prices; box size and gross weight (and whether
they can pack it smaller); dropshipping with blind shipping (no invoice or ads in the box); custom
logo or packaging and its minimum order; relevant test reports; and permission to use their
product photos and videos.

## 5.3 Shipping options to US shoppers

| Option | Cost signal | Transit | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Duties-paid (DDP) small-parcel lines** | 0.5 kg about $11–18; 1 kg about $26 on one line ([NextSmartShip](https://www.nextsmartship.com/blog/ship-from-china-to-us/), [Fishgoo](https://blog.fishgoo.com/china-shipping-cost-estimator/)) | 7–13 business days | Testing products; light items under about 0.5 kg billed |
| **CJPacket** (via CJ) | Roughly $2–6 for small items | about 7–15 days | You source through CJ |
| **Bulk to a US warehouse (3PL)** | Freight per kg + duty on *your purchase price* + pick & pack about $3.20 ([Fulfill.com](https://www.fulfill.com/3pl-pricing)) + US postage (1 lb zone 5 commercial about $8.74 after Apr 2026) | 2–5 days to the shopper | Proven products; heavier items; TikTok Shop US |
| **Fulfilled by TikTok** | Reported from $3.58–4.28 per unit + storage ([AMZ Prep](https://amzprep.com/tiktok-shop-fulfillment-fees/)) | Fast | Selling on TikTok Shop US with US stock |

**USPS details:** since 12 Jul 2026, Ground Advantage commercial prices under 1 lb are the same
within a zone regardless of ounces ([Pirate Ship](https://support.pirateship.com/en/articles/15453569-july-2026-usps-rate-and-rule-changes)),
and peak-season surcharges apply from October ([ShipEngine](https://help.shipengine.com/hc/en-us/articles/24275655418011-USPS-2024-Rates)).

### When to move stock to a US warehouse

Move when **all three** are true:
1. A product sells steadily (for example 10+ orders a day for 2 weeks; adjust to your numbers)
2. The [cost model](02-cost-model.md) shows bulk import is clearly cheaper per order, which it
   usually is above about 0.5 kg billed weight
3. You can afford the stock (100–500 units) plus warehouse minimums (often $500–2,500 a month, per
   2026 3PL pricing guides such as [Catalist](https://catalistai.com/blog/3pl-fulfillment-cost-breakdown-2026/)
   and [Fulfill.com](https://www.fulfill.com/3pl-pricing)), and you can handle
   import paperwork, including **CPSC eFiling** if the product falls under a CPSC rule (see
   [guide 6](06-legal-tax-payments.md))

## 5.4 Cut the billed weight

- Volumetric weight = L × W × H (cm) ÷ 6000 (or ÷ 5000). The desk perch's 37 × 25 × 7.5 cm box
  is billed as 1.16 kg although it weighs 0.875 kg.
- Ask for **flat-pack or compressed packaging** (frames apart, soft parts vacuum-bagged), poly
  mailers instead of boxes for soft items, and bubble pouches for jewelry.
- Bundle in one parcel: a 2-pack costs far less to ship than two 1-packs.

## 5.5 Packaging and unboxing

- An insert card with your brand, a care tip and a "share your video" ask with your TikTok handle
  (don't offer rewards for reviews, which can breach FTC rules)
- Gift-ready packaging for jewelry (pouch + card), which supports "giftable" messaging
- No supplier invoices or Chinese price tags inside ("blind shipping")

## 5.6 Returns, damage and lost parcels (shipping from China)

- **Low-value items:** offer a refund or reship without asking for the item back. Return postage
  to China costs more than the item. Ask for a photo as proof of damage.
- Budget refunds in the [cost model](02-cost-model.md) (3–5% for rigid, no-size items).
- Pick shipping lines that compensate for lost parcels, and keep tracking numbers on every order.
- Upload tracking to Shopify the same day, so customers get updates automatically.

## 5.7 Order flow (shipping each order from China)

```
Order on Shopify → (app or manual) order placed with supplier / CJ → supplier ships within 1–3 days
→ tracking number back into Shopify → customer email → delivered 7–13 business days
→ follow-up message asking for honest feedback (never conditional on a positive review)
```

Check fulfillment every day: any order not shipped within 3 business days gets chased with the
supplier the same day.
