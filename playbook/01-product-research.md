# 1. Product research

How to find products that sell on TikTok **and** still make money when you ship from China.
Most "trending product" lists ignore the second half, which is where most dropshippers lose money.
A cat desk perch is a good example: it's trending, but its 1.2 kg billed shipping weight wipes
out the profit (see [cost model](02-cost-model.md#example-b-cat-desk-perch)).

## 1.2 Hard filters (fail any one and drop the product)

For **direct shipping from China** to US shoppers:

1. **Billed weight above about 0.5 kg.** Billed weight is the larger of actual weight and
   volumetric weight (L × W × H in cm ÷ 6000). Above 0.5 kg, direct shipping usually eats the
   margin. Heavier products need bulk import (see [guide 5](05-sourcing-and-fulfillment.md)).
2. **Batteries, liquids, aerosols, powders or strong magnets.** These need restricted or dearer
   shipping lines.
3. **Regulated categories:**
   - supplements, food and drinks, cosmetics and skincare
   - children's products (CPSIA testing, and CPSC eFiling since 8 Jul 2026)
   - anything making medical claims
   - products that plug into the wall (electrical certification)
4. **Brand or IP risk:** "dupes", licensed characters, blind-box knockoffs, or anything with
   another brand's name.
5. **No margin room:** the best US retail price you can realistically charge is under 4× your
   landed product cost (product + packaging), or the competition sells it for less than your
   landed cost.

## 1.3 Scorecard (for products that pass the filters)

Score 1–5 on each line and multiply by the weight. Above 70 out of 100 is worth testing.

| Factor | Weight | 5 = | 1 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| Billed shipping weight | 20 | under 150 g | 400–500 g |
| Profit before ads at target price | 20 | ≥ $20 | under $10 |
| Shows well in a 3-second video | 15 | instant visible result (before/after, satisfying) | needs explaining |
| Demand evidence (tracker + search) | 15 | rising on 2+ sources | one source only |
| Bundle potential | 10 | natural 2–3 packs or add-ons | single item only |
| Returns and damage risk | 10 | rigid, no sizing (jewelry about 4% returns) | sizing or fragile |
| Competition and price pressure | 10 | few sellers, no $5 Amazon copies | flooded |

## 1.5 Validate before you scale

1. **Order 2–3 samples per product** and check quality, real weight and box size.
2. **Film 5–10 short videos per product over 7–10 days.** For each core video, try 3 different
   first-3-second hooks: a problem, the result, or a "POV" line.
3. **Watch these signals:**
   - How long viewers keep watching
   - Saves and shares
   - Comments like "where can I get this?" or "link?"
   - Profile and link clicks
4. **A product passes** when at least one video clearly beats your others, comments show buying
   intent, and the [cost model](02-cost-model.md) still leaves at least $15 before ads at the price
   you'd charge.
5. **Kill it** after about 10 videos with no standout. Move on; don't keep polishing a dud.

These pass/kill thresholds are working rules of thumb, not published benchmarks. Adjust them to
your own account's numbers.
