How it works

Licking is how dogs settle themselves. Give it a job.

A slow, steady lick keeps your dog focused on the mat instead of the water, the clippers or the thunder.

  1. 1

    Spread

    Smear a thin layer of something tasty into the grooves: xylitol-free peanut butter, plain yogurt or wet food.

  2. 2

    Stick

    Press the suction-cup back onto the tub wall, a tile or the floor at nose height. It stays put while your dog licks.

  3. 3

    Relax

    Wash, brush or trim while they work through it. Freeze the mat first and it lasts even longer.

Easy fill ideas

  • Peanut butter, xylitol-free only. Check the label: xylitol is toxic to dogs.
  • Plain yogurt or Greek yogurt, unsweetened
  • Wet food, spread thin across the grooves
  • Pumpkin purée, plain, not pie filling
  • Mashed banana, a little goes a long way

Treats should stay a small part of your pet's daily food. Ask your vet if your pet has a sensitive stomach or allergies.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will it stick to my tub?

It grips smooth, non-porous surfaces such as tile, glass, acrylic and enamel tubs, and stainless steel. Wet the suction cups and press firmly. It won't hold on textured, porous or painted walls.

Is it safe for my dog?

The mat is food-grade, BPA-free silicone. It's a licking mat, not a chew toy, so stay nearby, and take it away if your dog starts chewing it.

Does it help with anxiety?

Many owners use lick mats to keep their dog busy and focused during baths, grooming, storms and fireworks. It's an enrichment and distraction tool, not a treatment. If your dog has severe anxiety, talk to your vet or a qualified trainer.

How do I clean it?

Rinse it, then wash it on the top rack of the dishwasher or by hand in warm soapy water. A small brush gets into the grooves quickly.

How long does shipping take?

What if my pet doesn't like it?

You have 30 days to try it. If it isn't working for you, contact us for a full refund.

Does it work for cats?

Yes. Spread a thin layer of wet food or a lickable cat treat. Most cats prefer it on the floor rather than on a wall.
