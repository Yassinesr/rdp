Getting your cat to use it

  • Put it right next to your chair, at desk height, where your cat already likes to sit.
  • Add something that smells like you, such as a worn T-shirt or their favorite blanket.
  • Drop in a treat or some catnip and let them discover it on their own.
  • Lift them off the keyboard and into the perch each time, and give a treat when they stay.
  • Give it a few days. Cats warm up to new spots at their own pace.

Check the clamp is tight before each use, and don't go over the weight limit.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will it fit my desk?

It clamps onto desk tops up to about 6 cm (2.4 in) thick that have an edge sticking out, with room for the clamp underneath. It won't fit desks with a drawer, panel or frame right at the edge where you want to clamp it.

Will it damage my desk?

The clamp has padded grips, so there's no drilling and no marks on most surfaces. For delicate finishes, fold a cloth between the clamp and the desk.

Is it safe for a big cat?

It holds cats up to 9 kg (20 lb) when the clamp is fully tightened. Check it before your cat jumps in, and don't use it for cats over the limit.

What if my cat ignores it?

Try the tips above: put it right by your chair, add your scent and a treat, and give it a few days. If it still isn't working, you have 30 days to return it for a full refund.

How do I wash it?

Unclip the cover and machine wash it cold on a gentle cycle, then let it air dry. Wipe the frame with a damp cloth.
